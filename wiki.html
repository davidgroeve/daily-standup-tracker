User Manual

Welcome to the Rommaana Daily Standup Tracker. This platform is designed to streamline team communication, track long-term goals, and provide actionable insights into team velocity and project health.

Real-time Sync

All updates and changes are synchronized instantly across the entire team.

Data-Driven

Automatic metric calculation helps you understand team performance at a glance.

Secure

Your data is protected by enterprise-grade security and row-level access controls.

📊 Stand-up Grid

The Stand-up Grid is the heartbeat of your daily coordination. It shows what everyone is working on for the current week.

Key Features

  • Daily Updates: Click on any cell in the grid to add or edit your status for that day.
  • Week Navigation: Use the arrows in the header to jump between weeks or use the date picker to find a specific date.
  • Status Colors: Team members are color-coded to make the grid easy to scan.
  • Export: Use the "Export" button to download the current week's data as CSV, Excel, PDF, or a high-quality Image.

🎯 Goal Tracking

Located at the top of the Stand-up page, the Goals section tracks high-level targets that span multiple weeks.

Managing Goals

  • Creating: Click "Add Goal" to specify a target, owner, and deadline.
  • Status badges: Goals can be marked as "Pending", "In Progress", or "Done".
  • Visibility: Use the collapse icon to hide the goals section when you want to focus purely on the grid.

🗺️ Roadmap & Timeline

The Roadmap provides a macro-view of your project milestones and sub-tasks across a long-term timeline.

Timeline Features

  • Milestones: Large blocks representing major project phases. Drag ends to resize duration.
  • Sub-tasks: Granular items that can be nested under milestones.
  • Calendar View: Toggle to the Calendar view for a month-by-month breakdown of activities.
  • Visibility Filters: Use the "Select Items" filter in the header to declutter the roadmap and focus on specific projects.

📈 Analytics Insights

The Analytics dashboard transforms your stand-up data into powerful visualizations.

Core Metrics

  • Target Mastery: Measures how many long-term goals are being successfully completed within their deadlines.
  • Update Compliance: Tracks how consistently the team is providing their daily updates in the stand-up grid.
  • Availability: Shows the current team presence based on planned leaves and holidays.
  • Blocker Feed: A real-time stream of items tagged as blockers in the stand-up updates.

⚡ Activity Auditing

The Activity Log provides a transparent record of all changes made within the platform.

Using the Activity Log

  • Audit Trail: See who created, updated, or deleted any item, including goals, tasks, and stand-up entries.
  • Auth Tracking: Monitor user session activity (logins/logouts).
  • Filtering: Use the controls at the top to filter by User, Action Type, or specific Dates.
  • Grouping: Reorganize the list by grouping events by User, Action, or Date for easier auditing.
